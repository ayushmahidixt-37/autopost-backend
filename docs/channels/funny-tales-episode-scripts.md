# Funny Tales — Sample Episode Scripts (Batch 1)

Three full scripts written against the format in `gutli-kids-comedy.md`, to prove the joke structure before any production/automation work starts. Timings are targets for a ~60–75s Short.

---

## Episode 1: "The Independent Fort"

**0:00–0:03 — Cold open**
*(Dad stands in the living room doorway, staring at a blanket fort with a flag made of a sock taped to a ruler sticking out the top.)*
DAD: Baba... is that a flag?

**0:03–0:15 — Setup**
*(Flashback: 10 minutes earlier. Baba and Gutli under a blanket with a flashlight.)*
GUTLI: *(flat)* This fort has been standing for four minutes. That's basically a nation now.
BABA: Nations need a flag.
GUTLI: Then get me a sock.

**0:15–0:40 — Escalation**
*(Montage: Baba taping a sock to a ruler, Gutli "supervising" from a fixed sitting position, Baba solemnly reading a "declaration" off a cereal box.)*
BABA: *(dead serious)* By the power vested in me, the Republic of Blanket Fort... is now independent from the living room.
GUTLI: *(deadpan, to camera)* Nobody elected her. Democracy's dead.
*(Baba drags the couch cushions to "reinforce the border." Mom's coffee mug goes missing into the fort — "seized as tribute.")*

**0:40–0:55 — Punchline**
*(Back to present. Dad, holding the empty mug spot on the shelf, points at the fort.)*
DAD: Where's my mug?
*(Cut to inside the fort: the mug sits on a tiny "throne" made of Lego. Gutli sits perfectly still next to it.)*
BABA: *(innocently)* What mug?

**0:55–0:65 — Blame beat**
DAD: *(sighing, already walking away)* We are not doing this again.
*(Baba looks at camera, deadpan.)*
BABA: We're doing this again tomorrow.
*(Gutli, still frozen-toy pose, one button eye somehow seems to wink. No movement — just implied.)*

**0:65–0:75 — Outro tag**
GUTLI: *(to Baba only, once Dad's gone)* ...Worth it.
BABA: *(sighs)* Gutli, we talked about this.
*(End card: Funny Tales logo.)*

## Episode 2: "Science Fair Slime"

**0:00–0:03 — Cold open**
*(Baba's bedroom ceiling is dripping with green slime. Mom stands frozen in the doorway.)*
MOM: ...The science fair is tomorrow.

**0:03–0:15 — Setup**
*(Flashback: Baba at her desk with a "How Volcanoes Work" poster and a box of baking soda.)*
GUTLI: Baking soda and vinegar? Rookie. We need SCALE.
BABA: The assignment says "a simple demonstration."
GUTLI: The assignment is a suggestion.

**0:15–0:40 — Escalation**
*(Montage: increasingly large containers, glitter added "for scientific accuracy," a fan pointed at the volcano "for wind resistance testing.")*
GUTLI: *(narrating like a general)* Add the glitter. For SCIENCE.
*(The volcano erupts far bigger than expected, slime hits the ceiling, walls, the poster itself.)*
BABA: *(covered in slime, thrilled)* IT WORKED.
GUTLI: *(flat, also covered in slime, unmoving as a toy would be)* Obviously.

**0:40–0:55 — Punchline**
*(Present: Mom staring at the ceiling, slime still dripping in slow, satisfying drops.)*
MOM: How is there slime on the CEILING.
*(Baba looks up thoughtfully, genuinely unsure.)*
BABA: Physics?

**0:55–0:65 — Blame beat**
*(Mom pulls out her phone to take a picture "for insurance purposes." Baba beams like she's won an award.)*
BABA: *(to camera)* I'm getting an A.

**0:65–0:75 — Outro tag**
GUTLI: *(once Mom leaves)* ...Worth it.
BABA: Gutli, we talked about this.
*(End card: Funny Tales logo.)*

## Episode 3: "The Monster Under the Bed"

**0:00–0:03 — Cold open**
*(Baba tiptoes to the kitchen at night, arms full of cookies. Dad, in pajamas, blocks the doorway.)*
DAD: It is 11pm. Why do you have twelve cookies.

**0:03–0:15 — Setup**
*(Flashback: Baba under the covers with a flashlight, Gutli propped beside her.)*
GUTLI: There's definitely a monster under your bed.
BABA: *(terrified)* WHAT.
GUTLI: Relax. We just have to be diplomatic about it.

**0:15–0:40 — Escalation**
*(Baba slides a single cookie under the bed as an "offering." A soft rustling sound. She slides another. More rustling — friendlier this time. By cookie four, a small, harmless, fuzzy creature's eye peeks out, blinks, and retreats happily with the cookie.)*
GUTLI: *(nodding, satisfied)* See? Diplomacy. This is why I run foreign policy in this house.
BABA: *(delighted, whispering)* We're friends now.
GUTLI: We're ALLIES now. There's a difference.

**0:40–0:55 — Punchline**
*(Present: Dad still in the doorway, unconvinced, holding out a hand for the cookies.)*
DAD: There is no monster under your bed, Baba.
*(Cut to under the bed: a tiny, content, cookie-crumb-covered creature giving a thumbs-up to camera. Gutli, back in frozen-toy pose on the bed, one eye somehow angled knowingly at the camera.)*

**0:55–0:65 — Blame beat**
BABA: *(handing over exactly ONE cookie, keeping the rest firmly behind her back)* Fine. Here's one.
DAD: *(taking it, walking away, muttering)* I don't even want to know.

**0:65–0:75 — Outro tag**
GUTLI: *(once Dad's gone)* ...Worth it.
BABA: *(already sliding the rest under the bed)* Gutli, we talked about this.
*(End card: Funny Tales logo.)*

## What these scripts prove
- The 6-beat structure holds up across very different premises (fort/nation, science disaster, monster) without needing new lore each time — good sign for scalable, automatable generation.
- "...Worth it." / "Gutli, we talked about this." land naturally as a recurring closer in all three — worth locking as the permanent outro beat.
- Every episode's actual "danger" is imaginary/cartoonish (a friendly creature, spilled slime, a pretend nation) — nothing here should trip a content-safety check.
